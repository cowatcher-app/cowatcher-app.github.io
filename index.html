CoWatcher Extension 观影小助手 CoWatcher Extension

不用等待明天。现在就可以在 Chrome 与 Firefox 浏览器中使用它。无论是啃生肉日剧、英美小众剧集，还是在 YouTube / B站 进行外语沉浸，它都能为你带来原生级的智能双语体验。 No waiting required. Available today for Chrome and Firefox. Whether streaming raw Japanese drama, British television, or YouTube videos, enjoy AI dual subtitles and seamless vocabulary acquisition.

双模型协同 AI 字幕 (Fast + Supervisor)

Dual-Model Pipeline (Fast + Supervisor)

由轻量模型负责毫秒级低延迟出字，搭配高推理模型小步微批审阅语境。动态提取并校准角色全名、昵称与尊称（如将 Shou-chan 统一校正为“西宫硝子”），跨集连播自动继承，告别人名漂移。

Fast lightweight translation model delivers instant stream subtitles, paired with a reasoning supervisor model maintaining character glossary consistency across auto-playing episodes.

划词查词 · 生词 Anki 流水线

Click Lookup & Anki Bookkeeping

鼠标点击字幕即可即刻查看发音与释义，不跳出播放页面。后台静默识别剧情高频生词并完成 bookkeeping，一键导出至 Anki 卡片盒。

Click any subtitle word to inspect explanations in-place without pausing the narrative. Seamless automated vocabulary bookkeeping into Anki.

Zero Tab Switching · 纯净浸润学习

全平台自动化 Scrobble 追踪

Cross-Platform Auto Scrobbling

告别在各个网站手动打卡或使用零碎记录工具。只要你在浏览器观看，时间、集数、进度自动沉淀，生成精美的观影热力图与时长统计。

Track viewing time, episode progresses and heatmaps across all streaming tabs seamlessly. Never lose track of what you watched.
